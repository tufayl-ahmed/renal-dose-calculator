FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free and there is no sign-up. Nothing you enter is stored on a server; recent checks stay on your device.

Where does the dosing come from?

From FDA drug labels (DailyMed / openFDA). Curated rules are extracted from those labels and can be verified by a clinician; drugs without a rule are looked up in the live label. Every card links its source.

Which equations does it use?

CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free) for eGFR and KDIGO category, and Cockcroft-Gault for creatinine clearance, with a choice of actual, ideal or adjusted body weight.

Can I use it to prescribe?

No. It is an educational decision aid. Always confirm against the official prescribing information, local protocols and a pharmacist.

Does it work offline or on my phone?

Yes. Install it from your browser's menu. Kidney calculations work offline; drug lookups need a connection unless the drug has a curated rule cached.
