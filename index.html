How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds

  1. 1

    Enter the patient

    Age, sex, weight and creatinine — or paste one line like 72 M 78 kg SCr 1.4.

  2. 2

    Add drugs

    Type a name, brand or shorthand (piptaz, vanco, doxy). Results appear as you go.

  3. 3

    Read band, dose and source

    Each card shows the renal band, the label dose, cautions, the full dose table and the DailyMed link.

Safety first

Built to be honest about uncertainty

A label that doesn't mention the kidneys is never shown as "no adjustment". When an indication or product changes the dose, the card says which one it assumed. When nothing reliable is found, it says so and links the label.
