Safety

Honest about what it doesn’t know

Every answer carries a source badge that says how far to trust it, and four rules hold on every result.

How far to trust an answer

Most certain

  1. Clinician-verified Reviewed and signed off by a clinician.
  2. Curated · draft Hand-curated from the label, pending review.
  3. Auto-extracted Renal table extracted from the label.
  4. Label logic Worked out live from the current label.
  5. AI summary May be wrong. Always open the source.
  6. Review source No reliable automatic answer.

Least certain

Clinician-verified today: meropenem IV and piperacillin/tazobactam IV. Every other answer is labelled for what it is.

Four rules on every result

  1. Silence is not “no adjustment”. A label that doesn't mention the kidneys is never shown as “no adjustment”.
  2. Assumptions are named. When indication or product changes the dose, the card says which one it assumed.
  3. Kidney context is never hidden. Dialysis and unstable creatinine are called out on every result.
  4. No answer rather than a guess. If nothing reliable is found, it says so and links the label.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free and there is no sign-up. Nothing you enter is stored on a server; recent checks stay on your device.

Where does the dosing come from?

From FDA drug labels (DailyMed / openFDA). Curated rules are extracted from those labels and can be verified by a clinician; drugs without a rule are looked up in the live label. Every card links its source.

Which equations does it use?

CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free) for eGFR and KDIGO category, and Cockcroft-Gault for creatinine clearance, with a choice of actual, ideal or adjusted body weight.

Can I use it to prescribe?

No. It is an educational decision aid. Always confirm against the official prescribing information, local protocols and a pharmacist.

Does it work offline or on my phone?

Yes. Install it from your browser's menu. Kidney calculations work offline; drug lookups need a connection unless the drug has a curated rule cached.
